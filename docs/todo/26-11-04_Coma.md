- In the ceiling analysis coma is a bucket: everything that breaks the 7-blade symmetry
- That includes Seidel coma, astigmatism, and cat's-eye vignetting
- Two simple stats are pure coma signal, same moments used for anisotropic (EWA) filter footprint:
    - Centroid: first moment, where the lights average position sits
    - Covariance ellipse: the second moment: how stretched the light is along the radial direction compared with the tangential one

- At the edge where the target is measured, coma is mostly a radial stretch - its strong in the core and moderate (about 20% wider radially) across the whole halo
- There is also a small shift, and its sign changes between the core and the halo
- Mid-frame the stretch is tangential only in the core

- A stretch along a known direction is exactley what a pair of taps +-offset along that direction produces: anisotropic filtering with a footprint the network controls. Making the pairs two weights unequal gives that shift also

Proposed design

- at each level, replace the 2-tap lerp with 4 taps mixed by a softmax (a function that turns any set of numbers into positive weights that sum to 1):

out = w0·mip(x) + w1·mip(x + o) + w2·mip(x − o) + w3·up(low)(x)
w   = softmax(MLP(uv)),   MLP<2, 16, 4>
o   = H · d̂   in this level's texels   (d̂ = unit direction from the frame centre)
